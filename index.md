# Privacy Policy for QuickWeek

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026

**Application Name:** QuickWeek – Weekly Planner

**Developer:** AZAPPS

QuickWeek the App respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use QuickWeek.

By using the App, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

QuickWeek is designed to help users organize their weekly schedules, manage daily tasks, and plan their activities.

### 1.1 Information You Provide

When using QuickWeek, you may enter information such as:

- Task names and descriptions.
- Daily and weekly schedules.
- Dates, times, and reminder settings.
- Personal preferences and application settings.

This information is used to provide the App's planning and task management features.

QuickWeek stores your planning information locally on your device.

We do not transmit your task names, descriptions, or personal schedules to our servers.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

QuickWeek uses third-party advertising services that may automatically collect certain information when you use the App.

Depending on your device settings and applicable privacy choices, this information may include:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Device information and advertising identifiers.
- App interactions and usage information.
- Technical and diagnostic information.

This information may be collected by advertising partners to provide advertisements, measure their performance, and prevent fraud.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed through QuickWeek is used to:

- Create, display, and organize your weekly schedule.
- Save and manage your daily tasks.
- Provide reminders and notifications.
- Remember your application preferences.
- Display advertisements and support the App's operation.
- Maintain the functionality, reliability, and security of the App.

Your personal task entries and schedules are not used by us for advertising purposes.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

QuickWeek uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions, and diagnostic information to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, and prevent fraudulent activity.

Depending on applicable laws, your location, device settings, and consent preferences, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google's information about advertising:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

QuickWeek does not control the independent data processing practices of third-party advertising providers.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Your tasks, schedules, and application preferences are stored locally on your device.

You can delete your planning information using the available task management features in the App.

You may also remove locally stored application data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Information collected by third-party services, including Google AdMob, may be retained according to their respective privacy policies and data retention practices.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal task entries or weekly schedules.

Information collected through Google AdMob may be shared with Google and its advertising partners for advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

## 6. Notifications and Device Permissions

QuickWeek may request notification permission to remind you of scheduled tasks and upcoming activities.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Notification permissions are used to provide task reminders and related application functionality.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through QuickWeek.

Your locally stored planning information remains on your device unless you choose to share it through an available device or application feature.

Information transmitted by third-party services is subject to the security practices of those providers.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage your information and privacy preferences by:

- Editing or deleting your tasks and schedules.
- Managing notification permissions through your device settings.
- Managing advertising privacy preferences where available.
- Resetting or deleting your Android advertising ID through your device settings.
- Clearing the App's locally stored data or uninstalling QuickWeek.

Where required by applicable law, advertising consent options may be provided within the App.

You may contact us using the details below if you have questions about your personal information or wish to make a privacy-related request.

## 9. Children's Privacy

QuickWeek is intended to be used in accordance with the applicable age requirements and privacy regulations.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected or processed inappropriately, please contact us using the information provided below.

We will review the request and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.
